3. Digital Markets and Digital Goods

Digital markets fundamentally transform traditional economic transaction models:

Economic Differences: Physical vs. Digital Goods

In physical commerce, marginal cost of production ($MC$) is consistently positive due to raw materials, fabrication, and packaging. In contrast, digital goods possess a marginal reproduction cost of zero ($MC = $0). Once the master copy of an e-book or software repository is created, streaming or downloading the $10,000^{\text{th}}$ copy incurs no additional manufacturing expense.

Furthermore, digital goods exhibit non-rivalrous consumption: simultaneous access by hundreds of thousands of users does not diminish the product's availability or quality.

4. E-Commerce Business Models

NovaCraft Digital adopts a Hybrid B2C & Creator Marketplace Model:

B2C Catalog (Direct Sales)

First-party proprietary digital goods developed in-house yield 100% gross profit margins, establishing quality benchmarks for the platform.

C2C Creator Marketplace

Verified independent instructors and software authors list assets, earning an 85% payout while the platform takes a 15% transaction fee.

Network Effect Multiplier

Creators bring their own audiences to the platform, driving down Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC) while expanding overall catalog variety.
